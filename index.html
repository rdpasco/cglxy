About This Training

This training provides practical skills for interpreting GWAS results and identifying candidate genes using CropGalaxy. Participants will navigate the interface, explore genomic datasets, and apply structured analysis workflows for post-GWAS candidate gene discovery.

The course is designed for graduate students, researchers, and early-career bioinformaticians who want hands-on experience with rice genomic data and integrated analysis tools.
