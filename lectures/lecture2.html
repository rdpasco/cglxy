Introduction

Welcome to Lecture 2 of the Post-GWAS Analysis Training course. In this session, we focus on applying CropGalaxy tools to post-GWAS analysis workflows, including region exploration, gene identification, and functional investigation.

Objectives

  • Understand the workflow of post-GWAS candidate gene identification.
  • Learn how to access and configure core analysis tools.
  • Explore how genomic regions are processed across reference genomes.
  • Understand dataset handling and workflow integration.

Lecture Content

We will begin with a review of a GWAS-identified QTL region and demonstrate how to refine the region using alignment tools. From there, we will extract candidate genes and retrieve their sequences for downstream functional interpretation.

The session includes a guided walkthrough of a complete post-GWAS workflow using CropGalaxy tools, preparing you for the upcoming hands-on exercises.

Next Steps

After completing this lecture, proceed to Exercise 1 to begin practical application of the workflow using real genomic datasets.
